# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers reviewing Yixin Cui for international business, overseas operations, global communication, content, and adjacent Hong Kong opportunities. A secondary audience is collaborators who want to understand her creative practice and self-directed digital projects. Visitors may have less than a minute, so the site must support both fast scanning and deeper exploration.

## Product Purpose

This is a personal portfolio and digital profile, not a web copy of a CV. It turns a broad set of academic, professional, and creative experiences into one coherent story: the different sides of Yixin are connected by language, cross-cultural communication, curiosity, and making. Success means a recruiter can identify her strongest evidence quickly, while an interested visitor can discover the fuller archive without losing the thread.

## Positioning

An interactive personal book in which one globally minded communicator appears as a learner, professional, and creator - with every claim connected to concrete work.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: email Yixin about an opportunity or collaboration.
- Secondary CTA: open or download the formal CV.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "I'm a student, a professional, and a creator."
- Belief ladder: Yixin has a distinctive point of view; her varied experiences form a coherent international profile; she has credible results; she can contribute across communication, trade, operations, and creative work; she is easy to contact.
- Proof on hand: a two-page English CV, a 33-page awards and certificates collection, quantified work outcomes, and verified creator metrics supplied in this workspace.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, curious, and worldly. The voice is concise and self-assured, with a light human warmth. The site should feel like opening an illustrated field notebook at golden hour: intelligent enough for recruitment, personal enough to remember.

## Anti-references

Do not build a generic developer portfolio, a LinkedIn clone, a dense resume page, a corporate card grid, a children's picture book, a game-like room, or a collection of unrelated hover effects. Avoid decorative glass panels, beige editorial templates, neon cyber aesthetics, and motion that does not clarify navigation.

## Design Principles

- Room as trailer, chapter as archive, case as evidence.
- Show, do not repeatedly instruct: characters are primary navigation and objects are optional clues.
- Let visitors browse like a book or jump directly like a recruiter.
- Motion supports orientation; optional sound is limited to paper turns and quiet object-handling feedback.
- Keep the content data-driven so featured work can change with the target role.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain usable by keyboard and touch, preserve readable contrast, expose semantic navigation labels, provide a reduced-motion mode, and never require sound. Chinese and English carry equivalent meaning but may use natural, non-literal phrasing.
